About us

We turn ideas into memorable stories and experiences.

Founded in 2020 and headquartered in Kigali, Rwanda, Zahabu Media Ltd. is a registered communication, media production, and event management company. We specialize in delivering strategic, high-quality, and socially impactful content that drives public awareness, behavior change, and community engagement.

From concept development to final production, we partner with private sector clients, NGOs, development agencies, and civil society organizations to build media and event solutions tailored to audience needs and project objectives.

Our mission

To blend creativity and strategy in high-impact media solutions that help clients communicate effectively and achieve measurable results.
